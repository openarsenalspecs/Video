# EpisodeMarket

**Break the channel. Buy the episode.**

## Overview

EpisodeMarket is an open-source atomic media commerce platform designed to replace traditional subscription-based content models with a creator-driven, episode-level marketplace.

Instead of forcing users into bundled channels, catalogs, or recurring subscriptions, EpisodeMarket treats each episode, series, and media asset as an independent economic unit. Creators, studios, and rights holders can define their own pricing models while users purchase only the content they choose.

EpisodeMarket creates a transparent content economy where:

- Episodes are the primary unit of commerce
- Creators control pricing
- Bundles are optional and dynamically generated
- Accessibility is universal
- Enterprise deployments remain flexible without returning to forced subscription models

Licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, EpisodeMarket is designed as open infrastructure for the future of digital media commerce.

---

# Core Principles

## Atomic Content Economy

EpisodeMarket is built around the principle that digital content should be individually addressable and independently valued.

The platform treats:

- Episodes
- Series
- Films
- Specials
- Bonus content
- Licensed media assets

as distinct content units that can be managed, priced, licensed, and distributed independently.

---

## Creator-Controlled Pricing

Creators and rights holders maintain control over how their content is valued.

Supported pricing models include:

- Individual episode pricing
- Full series pricing
- Season pricing
- Optional creator-defined bundles
- Promotional pricing
- Limited release pricing
- Premium content pricing

EpisodeMarket provides pricing infrastructure without forcing creators into a single monetization model.

---

## No Forced Subscription Model

EpisodeMarket removes subscription dependency from content access.

The platform avoids:

- Mandatory monthly content subscriptions
- Channel-based content locking
- Artificial content bundling
- Paywalls around accessibility features

Users purchase the content they want while creators maintain direct control over their catalog.
