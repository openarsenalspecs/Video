# Human Intent Governance Standard

Human Intent Governance Standard (HIGS) is a structured governance framework for AI systems that ensures every action begins with verified human intent, passes through legal and policy validation, and remains fully auditable, explainable, and human-controlled.

The system is designed for high-trust environments where compliance, risk management, and accountability are required by default, not as optional layers.

## Core Design Principles

* AI asks questions before making recommendations.
* Humans define intent.
* AI verifies legality, compliance, and policy alignment.
* AI presents options rather than making final decisions.
* Humans remain accountable for all final decisions.
* Every significant decision is explainable, transparent, and auditable.
