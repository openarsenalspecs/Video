# ImageWorks

**Built for Creative Teams and Production Workflows.**

ImageWorks is a modular, self-hosted, open-source visual asset management and production platform designed to help creative teams organize, process, analyze, collaborate on, and prepare images for commercial use. The platform provides configurable workflows, automation tools, asset management capabilities, quality analysis, and production-ready export pipelines from a unified workspace.

## Vision

ImageWorks is designed to manage the complete lifecycle of visual assets, from initial image generation and asset ingestion through processing, collaboration, production, export, and long-term archival. The platform is built around a modular architecture that allows organizations to enable only the features they need while remaining fully extensible through plugins and integrations.
